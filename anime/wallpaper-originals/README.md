# Bộ nền IC Study

Bốn ảnh mới được tạo bằng công cụ ImageGen tích hợp, sau phản hồi yêu cầu thay đổi dáng và thêm phong cảnh. PNG gốc nằm tại đây; bản WebP lossless cùng độ phân giải và thumbnail dùng trên web nằm trong `ic-study-online/public/assets/wallpapers`.

Prompt chung: original finished anime wallpaper, wide landscape 16:9, soft pastel colors with clear linework, no typography, watermark, collage or website mockup; adult fully clothed characters; avoid generic seated reading poses.

- **mochi.png — Mochi Hug:** kawaii close-up of a cat-eared woman hugging a round white plush cat against her cheek, puffed cheeks, pout, violet eyes, brown hair with blue clips; milk-pink background with little paw motifs; large face on the right, quiet space on the left.
- **sunny.png — Sunny Meadow:** reclining on the stomach in a sunny daisy meadow, feet bent behind, chin on one hand and waving with the other, wink and broad smile; honey-blonde bob, mint/cream casual clothes, small sleeping white kitten; low side-on full-body framing.
- **ocean.png — Ocean Journey:** landscape without people; cream/turquoise train on a bridge over clear sea, coastal village, distant island, fluffy clouds and hydrangeas; broad airy panorama in aqua/cyan.
- **starlight.png — Starry Village:** landscape without people; rounded hillside cottages, large crescent moon, starry periwinkle/lilac sky, lantern path, reflective stream, little sleeping white cat; warm windows and soft hand-painted scenery.

Theme gợi ý tương ứng: Mochi Pink, Sunny Cream, Ocean Breeze và Starry Night. Có thể bỏ chọn đổi theme cùng ảnh trong thư viện trên web.
